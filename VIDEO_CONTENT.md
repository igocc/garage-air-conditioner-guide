# Garage AC video content package

## Scope

These are website explainer storyboards, not finished video files and not social-platform publishing packages. The page previews each script beat interactively in a 16:9 stage. No autoplay audio, TTS, upload, or synthetic presenter is implied.

The retention structure follows: **0-2s hook → 2-5s confirmation → payoff beats → one CTA**. The full production strings used by the page live in `src/videoContent.ts`.

## Episode 01 · Why bigger BTU is not Step 1

Primary hook family: contrarian claim.

Hook options:

1. “A bigger air conditioner may be the wrong first fix for a hot garage.”
2. “If the garage leaks heat everywhere, more BTU only hides the first problem.”
3. “Before you buy capacity, find where the cool air escapes.”

Payoff: door and ceiling envelope, sealing, heat-rejection path, then load band.  
CTA: use the garage planner before comparing products.

## Episode 02 · Portable AC or mini-split?

Primary hook family: named constraint.

Hook options:

1. “Portable does not mean ventless.”
2. “The right system starts with one question: where can the heat leave?”
3. “Rental, daily office, or weekend gym? The answer changes.”

Payoff: permitted opening, occasional versus daily use, DOE/SACC comparison, permanent wall route.  
CTA: compare products on the same rating basis and verify the exact manual.

## Episode 03 · Heat, humidity, and fumes are three different jobs

Primary hook family: stakes and safety.

Hook options:

1. “Cooling polluted air does not make it safe.”
2. “One machine cannot be your AC, dehumidifier, dust collector, and fume exhaust.”
3. “The thermostat is only one of the garage numbers that matters.”

Payoff: temperature, relative humidity, dust collection, local exhaust, and no-fit output.  
CTA: stop the product recommendation until source control is resolved.

## Episode 04 · Operating range is not winter capacity

Primary hook family: risk reversal.

Hook options:

1. “Below-zero operation does not prove enough heat on the coldest night.”
2. “A heat pump can still run while your garage needs more heat than it can deliver.”
3. “Do not use a product-page temperature range as a freeze-protection plan.”

Payoff: heat loss, local design condition, capacity retention, COP, defrost drainage, snow clearance, and backup protection.  
CTA: treat the product as a shortlist until performance is verified.

## Accessibility and production notes

- Every beat has on-screen text for muted viewing and a matching spoken line.
- Keep captions separate from the large headline and inside the lower-middle safe area.
- Avoid rapid flashes and preserve the page's reduced-motion behavior.
- Product prices, ratings, voltage, and operating claims must be refreshed from official pages immediately before editing or publishing.

## AI-content disclosure

Use this line wherever the generated storyboard visuals appear in a finished edit:

> AI-generated illustrative visuals. Product specifications and installation requirements should be verified on the current official product page.
